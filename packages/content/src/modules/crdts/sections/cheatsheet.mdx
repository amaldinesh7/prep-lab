**Remember this:** CRDTs are a structural answer to "converge without a coordinator." Yjs is what most teams ship. Yjs gives you that for free in return for ~3× memory, an immutable block model, and the discipline to handle GC, persistence, and schema evolution thoughtfully. When all of that matches your product, it's the right call. When it doesn't, last-write-wins on Postgres is still a great tool.
