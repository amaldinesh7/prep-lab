Keep this open while you ship. Half of perf work is remembering to do the boring thing — image dimensions, font-display, the right import path.

<Cheatsheet rows={[

**Remember this:** Profile before you optimize. The metrics that matter are LCP, INP, CLS at p75 of real users. Bundle, virtualize, lazy-load, reserve space — in that order, that's most of the wins.
