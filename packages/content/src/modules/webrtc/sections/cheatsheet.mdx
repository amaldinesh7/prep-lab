**Remember this:** WebRTC is heavy. The signal-to-noise ratio is low until you've internalized the three pillars and the NAT story. Once you have, it's the only browser API that does what it does — and for collaboration, calling, and games, the alternatives are worse. For everything else, WebSocket plus your server is faster to build and easier to operate.
